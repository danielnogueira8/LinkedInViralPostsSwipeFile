# SwipeIn Content Workspace

SwipeIn helps a person collect proven LinkedIn source material, turn it into original content with Cowork, and move finished drafts toward publication. This glossary names the concepts shared across the app, agent, and external connector.

## Ownership

**Workspace**:
The personal boundary that owns one person's content, sources, writing guidance, conversations, and publishing activity.
_Avoid_: Organization, team, account, tenant

**Creator**:
A LinkedIn author selected as an ongoing source of content inspiration.
_Avoid_: Account, profile, tracked account

## Source Material

**Source Post**:
A LinkedIn post collected from a Creator and qualified for the Swipe File.
_Avoid_: Viral post, swipe item, scraped post

**Bookmark**:
A LinkedIn post deliberately saved into a Workspace's bookmark library, whether or not its Creator is tracked.
_Avoid_: Saved post, favorite

**Bookmark Share**:
A deliberate grant that lets another Workspace use the owner's Bookmark library without transferring the Bookmarks or Workspace membership.
_Avoid_: Team access, shared Workspace, ownership transfer

**Model Source**:
The specific source material selected to guide the structure or approach of a Cowork deliverable.
_Avoid_: Reference, inspiration, attachment

## Creation

**Cowork Turn**:
One user instruction and Cowork's resulting response, including any clarification, actions, or deliverables.
_Avoid_: Request, run, generation

**Artifact**:
A deliverable presented inside a Cowork conversation, such as a post, hook, or citation card. An Artifact is not a board item until the user saves it as a Draft.
_Avoid_: Draft, message, output

**Draft**:
Editable content saved to the Posts workflow, where it can move from idea through drafting and readiness to publication.
_Avoid_: Artifact, post row, board artifact

**Content Template**:
A reusable structural pattern for writing content without carrying the facts of a particular source post.
_Avoid_: Format, example post, model source

**Custom Skill**:
Workspace-authored task guidance that changes how Cowork approaches a selected turn.
_Avoid_: Prompt, command, template

**Voice Profile**:
The Workspace's durable description of its own writing tendencies.
_Avoid_: Creator Style, tone prompt

**Creator Style**:
A reusable description of how a selected external Creator writes.
_Avoid_: Voice Profile, template

**Lead Magnet**:
A standalone resource offered to readers through a LinkedIn post, typically in exchange for a comment or direct-message interaction.
_Avoid_: Giveaway post, attachment, document post

## Publication

**Plan Date**:
The calendar day on which a Draft is intended to be posted, without implying that automated publishing is scheduled.
_Avoid_: Schedule date, publish date

**Scheduled Draft**:
A Draft with an accepted automated publishing instruction for a specific instant.
_Avoid_: Planned Draft, queued post
